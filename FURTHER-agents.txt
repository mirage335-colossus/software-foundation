path. Existing preflight dependencies, final evidence summaries and checked agent
helpers remain preferable to duplicate diagnostics/reporting/bookkeeping layers.


Test parallelism follow-ups (separate discussion)
-----------------------------------------------

- Implemented: automatic test/planner/tooling workers use CPU/RAM capacity,
  preserving affinity/container limits, explicit overrides, CPU/RAM headroom,
  shared-resource locks and the complete inventory. Parallel parents scope nested
  worker allowances instead of letting each child claim the entire machine.

- Parallelize the slow Python suites with isolated worker processes, using existing
  timing receipts to select useful partitions rather than spawning a process for
  every small test. Extend tools/run_tests.py and the existing inventory/aggregation
  mechanisms while preserving module/class fixture semantics, per-case results and
  explicit platform exclusions. Reject missing, duplicate, skipped or failed cases;
  retain bounded execution, descendant cleanup and complete diagnostic evidence.
  Deferred after measuring five slow suites: the two dominant suites spend 92%
  and 97% in one class; balanced suites offer about three seconds of theoretical
  class-level savings. Existing whole-suite scheduling remains the useful first
  step. Module fixture registration was repaired and regression-tested separately.

- Implemented: independent Linux source/recovery/archive/ABI batch cases share
  verified read-only inputs and prepared images, with private writable outputs,
  containers, temporary resources and displays. Output handoff requires confirmed
  container shutdown; all failures aggregate. Native Windows/host-browser and
  package-manager operations remain ordered. Source/recovery evidence stays separate.


Release discovery resilience (implemented)
-----------------------------------------

- Validated release IDs repeated across distinct pages can restart the complete
  initial inventory at most twice. Transport and inventory retries share the
  existing attempt/time/wait controls, plus a 768-request total ceiling and
  256-page per-inventory ceiling. Every prior page is discarded on restart.
  Persistent repetition, same-page duplicates, malformed rows/links, access
  failures and genuinely ambiguous tags still fail. Focused tests cover recovery,
  exhausted budgets, discarded partial matches, unchanged publication safeguards
  and zero extra calls/waits on first success. Exact-ID final publication/native
  acceptance readback from c45f8f3 remains unchanged. Recovery is read-only.
